About the Quiz

PURPOSE OF QUIZ

The Bertram "Bertie" Marshall Quiz and Spelling Bee is designed to keep the history and science of the steelpan alive for future generations.

Participants are tested on their knowledge of steelpan pioneers, pan construction, musical literacy and spelling, and general steelpan history and culture.

The Legacy of Bertram Marshall
