Bertie Marshall

1936-2012

Hometown: Laventille, Port of Spain

Profession: Welder

Second Captain of Hylanders Steel Orchestra from 1959

Click to Reveal
Key Innovations & Inventions
  • Harmonic Tuning (1956): Tuned notes by octaves and overtones, giving the pan its distinctive bright tonal quality.
  • First to electronically amplify the steelpan.
  • Helped develop the Double Tenor pan with Vincent Pompey.
  • Brought steelpan into churches to accompany hymns.
  • Awarded Chaconia Medal (Gold) 1972.
  • Awarded the Order of the Republic of T&T 2008.
Dr. Elliot "Ellie" Mannette

1927–2018

Hometown: Woodbrook, Port of Spain

Known as the "Father of the Modern Steelpan"

Click to Reveal
Key Contributions
  • First to use a 55-gallon oil drum for steelpan construction.
  • Standardized note layout for the tenor (soprano) pan.
  • Moved to the USA in 1967; settled at West Virginia University.
  • Founded Mannette Musical Instruments, training dozens of master tuners.
  • National Heritage Fellowship Award (NEA) 1999.
  • Percussive Arts Society Hall of Fame 2003.
  • Awarded the Chaconia Medal (Silver) in 2000.
  • Awarded the Order of the Republic of Trinidad and Tobago in 2008.
  • Recognized by the Smithsonian Institution of Washington, DC.
